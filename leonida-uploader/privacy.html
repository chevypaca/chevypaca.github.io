Privacy Policy — Leonida Uploader

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Leonida Uploader is a personal tool operated by its owner, Alejandro Pinto, to manage his own YouTube channels (Archivos Leonida, Leonida Files) and his own TikTok accounts. It is not a public service and it is not offered to third parties.

What the application accesses

Only with the account owner's explicit authorization (OAuth consent), the application may:

  • YouTube — administer the owner's account (upload, edit or delete his videos), upload videos, and read channel data, using the scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly.
  • TikTok — read the owner's basic profile information and statistics (user.info.basic, user.info.stats), read the list of the owner's published videos (video.list), and upload video drafts to the inbox of the owner's own accounts (video.upload). Every TikTok post is reviewed and published by the owner himself inside the TikTok app.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used solely to operate the owner's channels: uploading and scheduling videos, sending drafts to his TikTok inbox, and verifying that publications succeeded.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. No data about any other person is collected: the application only ever accesses the owner's own accounts.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time from Google account permissions or from TikTok Settings → Security and privacy → Manage apps.
