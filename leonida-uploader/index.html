Leonida Uploader

Leonida Uploader is a personal, owner-operated tool used by a single creator to manage his own YouTube and TikTok channels about GTA VI ("Archivos Leonida" and "Leonida Files").

With the account owner's own authorization, the tool can:

  • Upload videos and schedule their publication on the owner's YouTube channels.
  • Upload video drafts to the inbox of the owner's TikTok accounts, so the owner can review and publish each post himself inside the TikTok app.
  • Read the owner's published videos and channel statistics to verify that publications succeeded.

The tool is not a public service and is not offered to third parties. It only ever accesses accounts whose owner has explicitly authorized it, and that authorization can be revoked at any time.
